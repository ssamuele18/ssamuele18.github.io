ITA18 is the discrete-time Ground Motion Model used by Italian seismologists. This project extends it to the functional domain with improved functional regression techniques, focusing on bootstrap confidence intervals for the covariates of the continuous-time model: insight that matters for seismologists and, ultimately, for the safety of buildings.
